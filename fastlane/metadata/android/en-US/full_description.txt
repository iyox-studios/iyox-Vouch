iyox Vouch is a clean, modern debt and expense tracker designed to help you keep track of money lent to or borrowed from friends, family, and groups.

Features:
• Quick Single & Group Vouches: Split bills and expenses easily
• Multi-Currency Support: Custom currencies with automatic formatting
• People & Settled Spaces: Clear history of settled balances
• Privacy First: Local storage with optional biometric lock and encrypted backups
• Beautiful Material 3 Design: Dynamic colors with dark and light themes

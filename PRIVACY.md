# Privacy policy - iyox Vouch

Last updated: November 13, 2026

## Who this policy covers

This policy describes data handling in iyox Vouch (Android package com.iyox.vouch), an open source debt and IOU tracker. The developer can be contacted at zoligosa.ewu@gmail.com.

## Data stored on your device

Vouch stores the names you enter, debt and expense amounts, descriptions, dates, group participants and settlement status in its local database to provide debt tracking. App preferences and widget configuration are also stored locally. These records may contain personal and financial information about you and other people.

The app works offline and has no accounts or advertising. It includes no analytics SDK or in-app crash-reporting service. It does not send your records to the developer or operate a developer-hosted sync service. It does not request access to your camera, contacts or location.

## Google Play diagnostics

Separately from the app, Android and Google Play may collect usage and diagnostic information according to your device settings and consent, including through Android vitals. The developer can view diagnostic reports and aggregated app-quality metrics in Play Console to investigate crashes, unresponsive behavior and performance issues. Reports can include technical information such as crash stack traces and device details. This platform-level reporting is handled by Google under its privacy policy and your Android and Google settings; it is separate from any analytics or crash-reporting SDK embedded in Vouch.

## Android backups and device transfers

Android system backup is enabled for the app. Depending on your device, backup provider and system settings, Android may copy app data, including its database and preferences, to your configured backup service or transfer it to another device. This happens through the operating system, not a Vouch server. The backup provider's terms and privacy policy apply. You can manage system backups in your device settings; the developer cannot access or delete these copies for you.

## Files you import, export or share

Vouch reads backup files you choose to import and writes backups to a location you choose through Android's file picker. A selected location may be on your device or managed by a cloud storage provider. Exported .iyxv files contain readable JSON and are not encrypted by Vouch.

When you use a sharing feature, Android passes the selected content to the app or recipient you choose. That content can include names, amounts and other financial details. Sharing images may be temporarily stored in the app's cache. Only share records with recipients you trust. External apps and storage providers handle received data under their own policies.

## Biometric authentication and widgets

The optional app lock uses Android's biometric authentication system. Vouch receives an authentication result; it does not receive or store fingerprint images, face images or biometric templates. This lock is an access control and does not encrypt the database or exported files.

If you add home screen widgets, your launcher displays their information, including names and balances, outside the app even when the app lock is enabled. The app lock protects access to the app; it does not hide or restrict widget content. Anyone who can view your home screen may see this information without authenticating in Vouch. Remove those widgets if you do not want financial information visible on your home screen.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Settling or archiving a record does not delete it. Clearing app storage removes local records and preferences. Android may restore previously backed-up data when you reinstall or transfer the app.

Deleting local data does not delete exported files, copies shared with others or system backups. Manage those separately with the relevant recipient, storage provider or Android backup settings. The developer has no copy of your app database to retrieve, change or erase remotely.

## Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any attachments are received and processed to answer your inquiry. Email is handled by the email providers involved, including Gmail for the contact address above. Please avoid sending financial records or other private information unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any applicable legal obligations. You may use the contact address to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence you have sent.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data handling changes. The date above identifies this version. The app includes a copy of the policy with each release.
